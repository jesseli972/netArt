Digital Drift

This short piece reflects on how we move through the internet—scrolling, clicking, pausing, and forgetting. It explores how attention fragments over time, and how meaning can still emerge from repetition and noise. The web feels infinite, but each moment we spend online is brief and easily lost.

Stanzas

We refresh the page
again and again,
searching for something new
even when we don’t know what it is.

Links lead to links, and paths multiply.
A single click becomes a journey,
and suddenly an hour is gone.

Things We Notice

  • Loading bars
  • Notifications
  • Endless scrolling

Things We Forget

  1. Why we opened the tab
  2. What we were looking for
  3. How long we’ve been here
