not now

You close the app.

The screen returns to your home screen. The little icon sits there, ordinary, waiting, as if nothing happened in it just moments ago. As if a draft doesn't still exist somewhere in the machine, holding the exact words you couldn't finish.

You tell yourself: later. You tell yourself: when I know what I actually want to say. You tell yourself a lot of things that mostly function as postponement.

Later is a room with no walls.

You put the phone in your pocket. You leave the room you were in. The message stays where it is, unsent, which is different from gone.

You put it away and let time pass.

At some point you reopen the app. The draft is still there.

you wonder if it knows you left
