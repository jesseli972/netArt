You wrote it five times. None of them were right. None of them were wrong, exactly, either. They were just not the version that would say it, and somewhere in the typing you understood that no version would, and you stopped typing.

The strikethroughs are visible to no one but you. To the machine they are just text that didn't survive. To the other person they don't exist at all. They live in a place that is technically real but functionally nowhere — somewhere between intention and record, where most of what you almost say lives.

You wonder how many drafts they have, on their end. How many "I"s with nothing after them.

You close the app again.

You go back to the message.
