checking

Still nothing.

You knew there would be nothing. That's why you checked. So the knowing could become official, documented, confirmed. Something about uncertainty is worse than the answer, even the bad answer. Even the silence that means silence.

The message sits there, small and delivered, beneath the timestamp. It is so clearly a message that wanted something back. You can see it. You wonder if they can see it too.

Maybe they're asleep. Maybe they're in the middle of something. Maybe they looked at it and didn't know what to say, the same way you didn't know what to say when you wrote it. The symmetry would be funny if it weren't so uncomfortable.

You check again.

You wait longer this time. You make a promise to yourself.

You decide it doesn't matter — or practice deciding.
