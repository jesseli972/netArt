You send it.

The little checkmark appears. Then another. Read receipts are off — you turned them off a long time ago, for reasons you no longer remember or maybe never examined closely enough to name.

Your phone stays silent.

You set it face-down on the table. You pick it back up. You set it down again. This takes approximately four minutes, which feels like a different unit of time entirely.

The waiting is its own kind of answer, you think. Or maybe that's just something you tell yourself.

You check again. Still nothing.

You put the phone down and try to let time pass.
