later

You put the phone down.

Not forever. You know that. But for now, for this particular moment, you place it on the counter face-down and go do something else. You wash a dish. You look out the window. You think about something that has nothing to do with the message, almost successfully.

Time passes the way it does when you're watching it: slowly, self-consciously, as though it knows you're paying attention.

An hour goes by.

Maybe two. At some point the kitchen gets dark and you turn on a light and that feels like an event, like a small proof that things are still happening in the world, that time is passing in the way time is supposed to pass.

You are learning something about yourself in all this waiting. You're not sure yet what it is.

You pick the phone back up.

You go to sleep without checking.
