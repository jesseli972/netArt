— a hypertext narrative —

The message
is still there.

You've read it four times. Maybe five. The words haven't changed but something about them keeps shifting, the way a sound does when you listen too hard.

You typed a reply once. Then deleted it. Typed it again, slightly differently. Deleted it again. Now the cursor just sits there, blinking with a patience you don't have.

It's a small thing. You know it's a small thing.

You could send it.

Or you could close the app and tell yourself you'll deal with it later.

Either way, the room is quiet. Either way, the phone is warm in your hand.
